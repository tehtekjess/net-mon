## Natural Earth 5.1.2

Made with Natural Earth. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
1:50m Admin 0 country polygons and representative country label coordinates.
Natural Earth vector data is public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The bundled world-map.json is a simplified derivative: exterior rings only,
0.16-degree simplification, Antarctica omitted. See data/README.md for the pinned
source, checksums and reproducible generation command. Map markers represent
country estimates, not precise IP locations.
